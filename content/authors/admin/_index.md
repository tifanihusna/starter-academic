Hello! I am Tifani, a Postdoctoral Fellow at the Interdisciplinary Research Center for Finance and Digital Economy, King Fahd University of Petroleum and Minerals, Saudi Arabia. I am an applied econometrician specializing in spatial econometrics, regional economics, and causal inference. My research investigates the socio-economic impacts of infrastructure development, labor market dynamics, and financial inclusion. Methodologically, I leverage advanced spatial modeling (such as Spatial Durbin Models and Synthetic Difference-in-Differences) to analyze geospatial and large-scale panel data. Some of my works have been published in reputable peer-reviewed international journals, such as Economic Modelling, Transportation Research Part A: Policy and Practice, and Bulletin of Indonesian Economic Studies.
